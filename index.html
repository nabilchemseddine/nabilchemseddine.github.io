Research Interests

∂
Nonlinear Elliptic PDEs

Study of existence, regularity, and qualitative properties of solutions to nonlinear elliptic equations.

Δ
Boundary Value Problems

Analysis of fully nonlinear elliptic PDEs with Dirichlet and Neumann boundary conditions.

∇
Variational & Topological Methods

Application of mountain pass theorems, Nehari manifolds, and genus theory to PDE problems.

∫
Critical Points Theory

Min-max methods and Morse theory for energy functionals in Sobolev spaces.
